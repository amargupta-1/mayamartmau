Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, open the cart drawer, choose Home Delivery or Store Pickup, and click "Send Order via WhatsApp". Your itemized list goes straight to our store desk.

What is the minimum order amount for home delivery?

Free Home Delivery in Mau is available on orders of ₹299 and above. For orders below ₹299, you can choose Store Pickup or confirm delivery charges with us on WhatsApp.

Are the prices on the website final?

We show our store selling rate alongside MRP where applicable. Final bill amount and any active bundle offers are confirmed before dispatch.

How do I get wholesale pricing?

Click "Request Wholesale Pricing" and send us a WhatsApp message with your item list and quantities for bulk rates.

Can I pay via UPI or Cash?

Yes! We accept UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm) and Cash both in-store and upon home delivery.
